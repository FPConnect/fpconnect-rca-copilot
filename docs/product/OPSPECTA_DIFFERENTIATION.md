# OPSPECTA — differentiation grounded in evidence

**Updated:** 2026-11-09
**Positioning to validate:** “Inteligência independente, rastreável e multimarcas para tecnologia em saúde.”

## What can be claimed today

- The code contains API-backed ticket workflows, equipment records, attachments, role checks, human-reviewed technical conclusions, a local knowledge-retrieval path and an exploratory priority heuristic.
- The public site currently offers a diagnostic reference scope (up to 100 service orders) and an indicators pilot reference scope (up to 300 service orders); timeline, data feasibility, security, acceptance criteria and commercial terms require scoping.
- The current homepage describes sample review, record quality and priorities for technical review without promising outcomes.
- The code does **not** yet prove multi-organization isolation, vendor-neutral integrations, validated predictive maintenance, measured savings, an operational agent team, or production-ready mobile fleet management.

## Defensible differentiation hypotheses

1. Start from authorized work-order history across permitted manufacturers rather than requiring continuous telemetry. This is a product hypothesis, not a delivered integration guarantee.
2. Make data quality and evidence lineage visible before operational metrics.
3. Distinguish observed facts, retrieved analogies and human-confirmed causes in the data model and UI.
4. Use small paid pilots to discover which indicators are reliable and valuable; publish results only with agreed method, sample, period, permission and limitations.
5. Keep connectors portable and model processing optional; avoid tying the roadmap to an agent or vector database trend without demand.

## Commercial validation questions

- Who owns, exports and authorizes the work-order data?
- What data fields exist and how many records are usable?
- What decision will a qualified reviewer make differently after the analysis?
- Which indicators can be independently reproduced?
- What security, residency, retention and deletion terms are required?
- Who pays, for which bounded deliverable, and what measurable acceptance criterion justifies renewal?

No pricing, market share, customer count or ROI figures are asserted here.
