## Findings and uncertainty

- The verified OEM examples are tied to the vendors' own equipment and service ecosystems. This suggests a customer-validation question around mixed-vendor workflows; it does not prove that no regional multi-vendor solution exists.
- Clinical-AI vendors address a different workflow and buyer problem from equipment lifecycle, service history and maintenance operations.
- Public product pages do not establish local deployment availability, API access, interoperability, commercial terms, performance or regulatory suitability.
- No market-size, adoption, pricing, market-share or ROI estimate is asserted.
- Product terminology and pages can change. Confirm capabilities directly with vendors before procurement or competitive claims.

## Product implications for OPSPECTA

1. Validate whether a hospital, distributor or clinical-engineering team has a specific mixed-vendor service-history problem and authority to share the required data.
2. Demonstrate value with a bounded, authorized import and reproducible service-history metrics before promising integrations or prediction.
3. Treat regional language, hosting, support, retention and regulatory requirements as separate discovery items; this scan does not resolve them.
4. Keep any cross-OEM positioning explicitly a hypothesis until named integrations and customer acceptance tests exist.
